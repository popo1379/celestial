### What You Need

To calculate your Sun, Moon, and Rising signs, you'll need three pieces of information:

1. **Birth date** (month, day, year)
2. **Exact birth time** (as precise as possible — check your birth certificate)
3. **Birth location** (city and state/country)

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** If you don't know your exact birth time, you can still find your Sun and Moon signs. The Rising sign changes every two hours, so it requires precision.

## Step 1: Find Your Sun Sign

Your Sun sign is the zodiac sign the Sun was traveling through on the day you were born. This is the most well-known placement in astrology and represents your core identity.

## Step 2: Find Your Moon Sign

Your Moon sign reveals where the Moon was positioned at your birth. It changes signs approximately every **2.5 days**, so you need your birth date — but not necessarily the exact time.

### Why the Moon Matters

The Moon governs your emotional nature, instincts, and inner world. While the Sun shows who you are, the Moon shows how you **feel** about being that person. It's the second most important placement in your chart.

## Step 3: Find Your Rising Sign (Ascendant)

Your Rising sign — also called the **Ascendant** — is the zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at your exact birth moment. This is the most complex of the Big Three to calculate because:

- It changes approximately **every 2 hours**
- It depends on your **exact birth time**
- It depends on your **birth location** (latitude and longitude)

### Why the Rising Sign Matters

The Rising sign shapes your outer personality — how you appear to others, your approach to new situations, and your physical bearing. It acts as a lens through which the rest of your chart is filtered.

### Manual Calculation (For the Curious)

If you want to calculate your Rising sign manually:

1. Look up the **Local Sidereal Time (LST)** at your birth location and time
2. Convert the LST to the **Right Ascension of the Ascendant (RA)**
3. Find which zodiac sign corresponds to that RA using an ephemeris table

## Common Questions About the Big Three

### Why don't I relate to my Sun sign?

It's common! Your Sun sign represents your core identity, but your Moon and Rising signs heavily influence how that identity expresses. A Leo Sun with a Virgo Rising might appear quiet and analytical at first — the warm Leo core is there, just filtered through a Virgo lens.

### Why do I need my birth location?

Your birth location (latitude and longitude) is necessary to calculate:
- The exact **Rising sign** (which sign was on the eastern horizon)
- **House cusps** (which areas of life each planet occupies)
- **Moon sign** with precision (the Moon moves quickly)

### What if I don't know my birth time?

If you don't know your exact birth time:
- You **can** still find your **Sun sign** (just needs date)
- You **can** estimate your **Moon sign** (needs date, time helps)
- You **cannot** accurately find your **Rising sign** (needs exact time + location)

Consider ordering a birth certificate from your birth location's vital records office — it's the most reliable way to get your birth time.
